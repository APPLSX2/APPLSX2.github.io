Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

A full PS2 emulator, with an app that stays out of the way.

No BIOS needed

A built-in HLE BIOS boots most games straight away. Have a BIOS dumped from your own console? Pick it in the BIOS tab for the best compatibility.

Built for speed

A PCSX2 core tuned for iPhone and iPad. When a heavy scene needs it, the PS2's CPU slows down just enough to keep the game at full speed.

Smooth motion

Frame generation adds in-between frames to games that run at 30 FPS, on devices that support it.

Two-player controllers

DualSense, DualShock 4, Xbox, Switch Pro and MFi controllers, with rumble and analog triggers.

Touch controls that fit

A full DualShock 2 on screen. Multi-touch and haptics, with adjustable size and opacity, laid out clear of the Dynamic Island.

Save anywhere

Five save-state slots per game, each with a screenshot. Memory cards for the game's own saves.

Your library, with covers

Box art appears on its own. To use your own picture, long-press a game and pick one from Photos or Files.

Sharper than the original

Up to 4× the PS2's resolution, plus widescreen patches for games that have one.

Works with Files

Import from iCloud Drive or anywhere in Files. You can also drop games, BIOS files and covers into Files › APPLSX2.

How it works

Three tabs. Three steps.

Games, BIOS, Settings. That's the whole app.

Import your games

ISO, CHD, CSO, BIN/CUE and more, from Files, iCloud Drive or your computer. Titles and covers fill in automatically.

Pick a BIOS (optional)

Keep the built-in HLE BIOS, or import one dumped from your own PS2.

Play

Use touch controls or a controller. Save and resume whenever you like, from the in-game menu.

Controllers

Grab a friend.

Two controllers, two players: the PS2's two pads.

  • Automatic players. The first controller is player 1, the next player 2. Swap them or pick one in Settings › Controllers.
  • Rumble. The game's vibration plays on your controller, big motor left and small motor right, like a DualShock 2.
  • Analog triggers. L2 and R2 are pressure-sensitive, as on the original pad.
  • One button to the menu. Home or the touchpad opens save, load and settings. If a controller disconnects, the game pauses.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does APPLSX2 come with games?

No. APPLSX2 doesn't include any games or BIOS files. Play games you own, dumped from your own discs.

Do I need a PS2 BIOS?

No. The built-in HLE BIOS runs most games. A BIOS dumped from your own console is the most compatible, and you can choose it in the BIOS tab. Save states only load with the kind of BIOS they were made with.

Which disc formats work?

ISO, CHD, CSO, ZSO, GZ, BIN/CUE, IMG, MDF, NRG and ELF.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later. Newer devices handle the most demanding games best, and some extras, like frame generation, need a recent iOS.

Can two people play together?

Yes. Connect two controllers: the first is player 1 and the second player 2. On a single controller, touch controls stay available for player 1 when the controller is assigned to player 2.

Does APPLSX2 collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
