Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

APPLSX2 is a PlayStation 2 emulator for iPhone and iPad. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

Your games, BIOS files, save states, memory cards, custom covers and settings are stored in APPLSX2's own storage on your device. You can see most of them in the Files app under APPLSX2. iOS may include them in your device backups, depending on your backup settings.

Network requests

APPLSX2 connects to the internet for one thing: downloading game covers. To show a game's box art, the app requests an image named after the game's serial number (for example SLUS-20370.jpg) from the community ps2-covers collection on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). That request contains the serial number and, like any web request, your IP address. GitHub's privacy statement applies to it. Covers are then cached on your device.

Some of the emulator runs in a built-in web view. That web view loads only from APPLSX2 itself, on your device, and sends nothing over the internet.

Photos

If you choose a custom cover from Photos, the system photo picker gives APPLSX2 only the picture you pick. APPLSX2 can't see the rest of your library. The picture is resized and saved in the app as the cover, without its location data.

Controllers

Game controllers connect through iOS. APPLSX2 reads their buttons and battery level, and sends vibration to them, only while you use the app.

Children

APPLSX2 doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/APPLSX2/APPLSX2.github.io.
